Why Consensus Room

Consensus Room was built for research, engineering, and writing workflows where one answer is not enough.

A single AI answer is often useful, but it can be too confident, too narrow, or blind to its own assumptions. Comparing several AI-generated viewpoints can reveal clearer disagreements, stronger caveats, and more practical conclusions.

Doing this by hand is messy: switching tabs, copying prompts, tracking rounds, and summarizing everything into a report. Consensus Room turns that process into a structured debate room.

You ask one question, several AI assistants respond, challenge each other, vote, and produce a final consensus report.

Consensus Room is for people who use AI for judgment, not just quick answers.

Privacy, trust, and responsible use

Consensus Room is designed as a local-first browser workflow. The extension does not collect passwords, cookies, or session tokens. Users control what content is sent to third-party AI services.

In Web Mode, users use their own AI accounts and are responsible for complying with each provider's terms, usage limits, and policies.

Consensus Room does not bypass paywalls, rate limits, CAPTCHAs, access controls, or safety restrictions.

If you enable API Mode, your API keys stay local in your browser storage unless a future version clearly says otherwise and asks for consent.

AI outputs may be inaccurate or incomplete. Please review results before relying on them, especially for professional, legal, financial, medical, investment, or compliance decisions.
